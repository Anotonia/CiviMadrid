# Art Direction — CiviMadrid v0.1

## Visual promise

A **beautiful readable strategy game**, not a spreadsheet dashboard wearing a hex-grid background. Reference mood: atmospheric table-top/isometric grand strategy map, warm golden-hour Mediterranean light, tactile tiles, high legibility. **Do not copy Civilization visual assets, icons, UI or trademarks.**

## First-screen composition

- **Center (dominant 70%+)**: 3D instanced tiles showing recognizable Madrid boundaries/river/parks, hills and dense city landmarks with consistent geometric scale.
- **Top HUD**: treasury / water / electricity / housing and turn index; changes animated on actual engine events.
- **Right contextual panel**: selected tile, sourced/derived information + legal build actions + cost, time, projected **modeled** consequences.
- **Lower rail**: construction queue, turn journal, compact timeline and big Advance Turn button.
- **In-scene feedback**: construction scaffolds, solar farm geometry, housing blocks, small flow markers; clear completed/under-construction states.
- **Attribution**: accessible source drawer, year, geography grain and truth class; don't mix with scenario numbers.

## Camera and control

Pan, orbit (optional bounded), zoom, hover/select, touch pinching and keyboard tile navigation. Camera default should show a cinematic readable overview; don't make player fight the camera. World cells and available actions need accessible companion HTML controls.

## Visual hierarchy and palettes

Neutral topography, restrained district colors, clear resource overlays. When overlay enabled, full legend and accessible category labels; color never only information. Typography and microcopy optimized for small screens. No photorealistic procedural "Spain" claim without geographic verification.

## Creative quality gate

A newcomer should understand **one meaningful action and its consequence within 90 seconds**. Instrument measured frame rate, interaction latency and mis-clicks. No false “AAA-level” status without render tests. Show visually attractive but honest placeholders with explicit SYNTHETIC notices until data arrives.

## Minimum visual evidence

Actual browser screenshot at 1440×900 and mobile viewport, short capture showing selecting a tile → legal build → advance turn → visible improvement + HUD change; accessible keyboard test and performance notes. A static promo illustration is not proof of gameplay.
